Two directions for laying accounts out side by side so the same stat is easy to compare. Real data from your two accounts. Toggle the theme to check both.

Option A

Account columns

Each account becomes a column. Smallest leap from today — metric rows line up across columns, so you scan left-to-right at the same height.

Option B

Comparison matrix

Metrics become rows, accounts become columns. Reading a single row answers "who's hottest on 5-Hour?" instantly — the leader is flagged. Best fit for your actual goal.

My recommendation

Option B (matrix) is the truer answer to "compare stats between accounts" — a row is one metric, so your eye never hunts. Option A (columns) is a gentler, lower-risk change and keeps each account's sparkline prominent. Both need a wider popover (~560pt for two accounts; it grows per account) — a real constraint on a menu-bar window worth deciding on. Tell me which direction (or a hybrid: matrix stats + per-account sparkline strip) and I'll refine this mockup before touching any SwiftUI.
